# View.Components.A11y

## Purpose

The accessibility toolbar rendered on every page: a launcher (Alt+A) and a modal panel with six profiles (seizure safe, low vision, ADHD, cognitive, keyboard, dyslexia), steppers for text size, line height and letter spacing, switches for readable and dyslexia fonts, title and link highlighting and left alignment, one color mode (dark, light, high contrast, monochrome, high or low saturation), and orientation aids (stop animations, hide images, mute sounds, reading guide, reading mask, big cursor, strong focus) plus a page map of headings and regions. Settings stay in the browser; nothing is sent to the server.
