# View.Components.A11y

## Purpose

The accessibility toolbar rendered on every page: a round blue launcher with a check badge (Alt+A) and a floating panel (blue header with close, language switch, title and Reset / Statement / Hide pills; cards below) with six OFF/ON profiles (seizure safe, low vision, ADHD, cognitive, keyboard, dyslexia), steppers for text size, line height and letter spacing, switches for readable and dyslexia fonts, title and link highlighting and left alignment, one color mode (dark, light, high contrast, monochrome, high or low saturation), and orientation aids (stop animations, hide images, mute sounds, reading guide, reading mask, big cursor, strong focus) plus a page map of headings and regions. Glyphs are Lucide outline icons (ISC, already credited for the app icons) on transparent backgrounds. Settings stay in the browser; nothing is sent to the server. The statement lives at `/accessibility` on the standard legal template.
