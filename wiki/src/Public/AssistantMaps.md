# AssistantMaps

## Purpose and contract
Progressively render server-projected bounded maps, ordered stops, shared-plan and navigation links. Validate paths/coordinates, label approximate location/travel, no road fallback invented. Abort and remove maps on close/reset, lazy load one at a time and retain readable text on failure.

## Grill Log
Resolved: bounded database-owned output, viewer privacy, free infrastructure, failure transparency and lifecycle tests required before delivery.
