# Privacy

Cookie choices on first visit (necessary always; analytics and marketing off until chosen), logged
as [[domain/Consent]]. Settings offer notification and marketing choices, location visibility,
attendee-list visibility, data export (JSON download), location-history deletion, pause, and
deletion with password confirmation (or a recent sign-in for Google accounts).
