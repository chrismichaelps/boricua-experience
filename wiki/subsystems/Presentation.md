# Presentation

Server-rendered pages in Spanish (default) or English, an app bar and a bottom tab bar on phones
(Explore, Map, Create, Activity, Profile), a top navigation on wide screens, the installable shell
(manifest, icons, service worker, offline page), structured data for events, a sitemap, and
`robots.txt`.
