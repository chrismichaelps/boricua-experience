# Account

- **Definition:** a member's profile (name, bio, home municipality, language), preferences
  (notifications, marketing, location visibility, attendee-list visibility), and status.
- **Status:** `active` or `paused`. A paused account is hidden, cannot join or publish, and shares
  no location; signing in offers reactivation.
- **Onboarded:** age (18+), terms, and privacy accepted. Required before any action.
- **Deletion:** upcoming events cancelled, location erased, the identity deleted, profile cascades;
  past events show "Deleted user"; consent rows remain unlinked as legal record.
