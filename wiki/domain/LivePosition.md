# LivePosition

- **Definition:** a member's latest position and accuracy while live location is on, written at most
  every 10 seconds; history sampled every 75 m or 5 minutes, kept 30 days, deletable at any time.
- **Visibility:** `only_me`, or `approved_activities`: visible to co-attendees and the organizer of
  an event the member is approved for, from three hours before it starts to an hour after it ends.
