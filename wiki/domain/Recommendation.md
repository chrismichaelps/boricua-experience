# Recommendation

- **Definition:** up to 30 upcoming public events with seats left, ranked by popularity (approvals,
  saves, likes, recent views, minus dislikes), the viewer's category taste over 180 days, distance
  when a position is known, and nearness in time. Own events, joined events, and disliked events
  are left out.
