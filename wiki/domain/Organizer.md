# Organizer

- **Definition:** the member who published an event. Shown on every event page with name, bio,
  home municipality, member-since date, events hosted, and guests hosted.
- An organizer whose account is paused or deleted appears without a profile link
  ("Deleted user" for deleted accounts; curated events have no organizer).
