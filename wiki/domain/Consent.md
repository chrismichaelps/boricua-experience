# Consent

- **Definition:** an append-only record that a subject (an account, or an anonymous browser id)
  granted or refused a use: terms, privacy, age, analytics cookies, marketing cookies, marketing
  messages; with version, source, and time.
- Strictly necessary cookies need no consent; nothing optional runs before a choice.
